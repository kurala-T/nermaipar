# NermaiPar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

NermaiPar is a student prototype. The Discord bot is named Nerman. This policy says what happens to messages you send it.

## What we receive

When you DM Nerman, or @mention it in a server, Discord gives the bot the text of that message so it can reply.

We do not ask for your password, OTP, bank PIN, or card number. Do not send those.

## What we do with the message

The bot reads the message to decide if it looks True, False, Misleading, or Unverified.

- Known scam examples are checked on the computer that is running the bot.
- If a free Google Gemma key is turned on, the message text is sent to Google's Gemini API so Gemma can check claims the local list does not know. Google's privacy rules then apply to that text: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms
- The bot sends the verdict back into Discord. We do not sell messages. We do not use them for ads.

Discord also stores messages under Discord's own privacy policy: https://discord.com/privacy

## What we do not do

- We do not sell your messages.
- We do not build a public profile of you.
- We do not keep a separate account database. The bot does not have its own signup.

## How long it stays

The running bot uses the message to write the reply. It does not save a copy of your chat into the NermaiPar project. Discord may still keep the message in the channel or DM.

## Contact

Message the Nerman bot on Discord, or contact the NermaiPar team through the VerseHack organizers.
