# NermaiPar Terms of Service

Last updated: 8 November 2026

NermaiPar is a student prototype for VerseHack 2026. The Discord bot is named Nerman. These terms cover the NermaiPar website and the Nerman Discord bot.

## What the bot does

You can send Nerman a forwarded message. It replies with a verdict: True, False, Misleading, or Unverified. It also names the trick in the message and gives you a short reply you can send back.

This is a prototype. It is not a bank, a doctor, a lawyer, or an official fact-checker. Check important claims with the original source before you act.

## Using it

- Do not send passwords, OTPs, bank PINs, or card numbers.
- Do not use the bot to harass people.
- You are responsible for what you paste into the bot and what you send back to a group.
- A verdict can be wrong. If the bot is not sure, it should say Unverified.

## The service

The bot runs when the NermaiPar team has it turned on. It may stop, change, or be taken down at any time. It is free for this hackathon. There is no paid plan.

## Contact

Message the Nerman bot on Discord, or contact the NermaiPar team through the VerseHack organizers.
