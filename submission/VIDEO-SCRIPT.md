# NermaiPar demo video script

Read this in English. Keep the whole recording between 3 and 4 minutes. Do not switch the website language. Leave it on English. The Hindi and Tamil labels in the language menu are the one app fix still to do before you record.

Record the screen and your voice together. Speak a little slower than normal. After you upload, set the link to anyone with the link, or Unlisted on YouTube.

## 0:00 to 0:25 — Who you are

"Hi. We are team Nermai TOPpers, and this is NermaiPar. The line under the name is: an omnipresent circuit breaker for digital misinformation. It helps a person pause a suspicious forward, see the trick, and reply without starting a fight."

## 0:25 to 0:55 — The problem

"In family groups, a scary message arrives already forwarded ten times. People share it because it sounds urgent, not because it is true. Checking it means opening another app, and by then the message has already spread. NermaiPar sits where the message already is."

## 0:55 to 2:10 — Telegram, the live checker

Open Telegram. Show the chat with @Nermaiparbot. Do not show any phone number.

"This is the live bot. I send it a real question."

Type: Did Nvidia get hacked this month?

Wait for the reply. Then say:

"It answers in a normal sentence, then stamps the claim True. Nvidia told customers that its Brev cloud platform had unauthorized activity from 4 October to 20 October 2026. It found no confirmed misuse."

Type: Nvidia gonhaked today, GPUs exploading

"The spelling is fixed, and this one is Misleading. The October breach was real. GPUs exploding was not. The bot also reacts to the message, so you can see the verdict without reading the whole reply."

## 2:10 to 3:05 — The website

Open the site in English. Stay on the mobile-app view.

"The website is the full flow: Detect, Explain, Respond, Train."

Click Health scam, then Analyze claim.

"Detect reads the forward. Explain shows the trick, like fake urgency. Respond gives a polite reply you can send back to an elder. Train is a short quiz so the next forward is easier to spot."

If you have time, tap one highlighted phrase, then show the reply box. Do not open Judge mode unless you are under 3 minutes 30 seconds.

## 3:05 to 3:40 — How it works, and what it cannot do

"A message goes to the bot. It fixes obvious spelling, searches current headlines, and asks Gemini for a verdict: True, False, Misleading, or Unverified. Weather questions use a live forecast. If the model is down, a small saved list still blocks the hoaxes we already know."

"This is a prototype. It can be wrong. It is not a doctor, a bank, or an official fact-checker. WhatsApp and SMS on the website are demonstrations, not live networks yet. Please confirm important claims with the original source."

## 3:40 to 3:50 — Close

"NermaiPar. Pause the forward. Protect the group. Thank you."

Stop the recording. If it is over 5 minutes, cut it. Judges only watch the first 5 minutes.
